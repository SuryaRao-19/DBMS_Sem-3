HOW THIS PLATFORM WORKS

End-to-End Academic EV Charging & Dual QR Workflow

1. Scan Station QR / Locate Hub

Inspect coordinate-verified hubs or scan a physical Station Identification QR placard (/qr/station/<token>).

2. Book Demo Time Slot

MySQL triggers validate zero overlap and lock your simulated bay slot cleanly.

3. Simulate Delivery

Stored procedure CompleteChargingSession calculates demo kWh, base tariff, and 18% GST.

4. Dynamic Payment QR & Razorpay

Generate a 10-minute expiring Dynamic Payment QR or launch Razorpay Test Mode Checkout with HMAC verification.
